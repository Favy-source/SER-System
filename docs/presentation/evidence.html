      The model reads arousal. It cannot read valence.

      Speaker-independent evaluation on three held-out actors. High-arousal emotions are recognised well; positive and low-arousal states collapse. This is the limitation the additional modalities exist to address — a smile carries valence that the voice alone does not.

          What is built, and what is proposed

          Status, stated precisely
          • Built & measured. Live audio and webcam capture, 468 facial landmarks over WebSockets, face tracked at 4 fps on GPU. Audio model evaluated speaker-independently.done
          • Built, not yet trained. Three fusion architectures — feature-level, decision-level, cross-modal attention. The trained checkpoint is audio-only.partial

          An emotion-recognition system should not be treated as an objective measurement of a person's internal state. Facial and vocal signals correlate with emotion; they are not identical to it. This is decision support for a professional — never a diagnosis, never a replacement.
